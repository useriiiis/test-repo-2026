# Stop Oversharing — EELC2010 Explainer Project

An interactive web + digital poster that persuades high-school students to stop oversharing on social media.

## Live demo

- **Web (Informative):** https://useriiiis.github.io/test-repo-2026/
- **Poster (Persuasive):** https://useriiiis.github.io/test-repo-2026/poster.html

| `style.css` | Shared cyber-security neon styling | — |

## Audience

Leo — an 18-year-old high school student who frequently posts on Instagram and Facebook, likes games and AI, and cares about maintaining connections while feeling safe.

## Science of Persuasion strategies used

- **Reciprocity** — we give a free privacy game and an AI chat first, before asking them to change settings.
- **Consistency** — we frame the behavior change as a small, consistent step ("you don't need to quit social media").

## Citations
